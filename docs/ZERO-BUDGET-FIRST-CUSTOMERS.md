# Qalam: first customers with zero cash budget

Date: 2026-09-14

## Starting point

Founder report: two people were asked to try Qalam; neither is using it now. No paying customers reported. Available weekly time is unknown. This plan assumes 30 minutes on five weekdays plus one 60-minute weekend session. Reduce recruitment volume if that is too much.

The next objective is five qualified people who try a real workflow, three who repeat it, and one who pays the existing published price. These are experiment targets, not forecasts or proof of product-market fit. A billion-dollar outcome cannot be promised.

## What the inspection supports

- The live homepage leads with LinkedIn publishing and saved voice context: https://www.byqalam.com/
- The live pricing page offers Solo at $10/quarter, Pro at $18/quarter, and Agency at $38/quarter. Agency requires reviewed onboarding: https://www.byqalam.com/pricing
- The repository includes writing, career tools, client workspaces, approvals, analytics and payments. Feature presence does not establish successful production use.
- Existing strategy documents already identify missing retention and payment evidence. More strategy documents or more features will not supply that evidence.
- `lib/marketing-events.ts` already defines signup, activation and payment events. `docs/METRICS.md` identifies durable activation and authoritative payment records. Production reporting was not verified in this inspection.
- Free currently includes five AI posts per month. Do not promise trained voice profiles, client approvals or publishing on Free. Those have paid entitlements.

## One hypothesis to test

Freelance LinkedIn ghostwriters with one to three active clients may value keeping client voice and revisions together enough to return and pay. This is a hypothesis based on the existing workflow, not proven market demand.

Recruit people who already deliver LinkedIn posts for clients and have recently experienced repetitive briefing or revisions. Exclude general AI enthusiasts and people merely collecting free tools. If the two existing testers are outside this audience, learn from their experience without treating them as evidence against this segment.

Keep existing career functionality available. For this experiment, show only the writing workflow. Do not redesign the whole homepage before hearing qualified customers.

## Days 1 and 2: learn why the first two stopped

Send individually after reviewing the wording. No messages have been sent by Codex.

> Hey, thanks for trying Qalam. I noticed you have not continued using it. No pressure to restart. What were you trying to get done when you tried it, and where did you stop? What did you use instead? An honest answer would help more than positive feedback.

Record their actual words. Ask for a specific recent task, not whether they like the idea. Categorize the answer as no recurring need, confusing setup, poor output, missing essential capability, price, or unknown. Silence remains unknown.

## Days 2 to 5: recruit five qualified testers

Find up to 15 suitable people manually using public service listings, existing contacts, and communities that allow product research. Prefer explicit requests for workflow help. Spend no money on lists, ads or tools. Do not scrape private contact details or automate invitations.

Use the Qalam brand through an account that is allowed to represent a business. Disclose your connection to the product. Do not invent a founder identity, employees, customers or endorsements. Public branding can avoid putting the founder in marketing, but it cannot guarantee anonymity.

Permission-first message draft:

> Hi [name], your [specific public example] caught my attention. I work on Qalam, a LinkedIn writing tool. I am researching how freelance ghostwriters handle client briefs and revisions. What part of that process was most time-consuming on your last project? If this is a problem for you, I can also send a short product walkthrough. No call needed.

Only use the personalization if it is true. Send through an appropriate permitted channel. One follow-up after four days is enough; stop after a decline or no response to the follow-up.

Follow-up draft:

> One follow-up on my note about client revisions. If this is not a current problem, no need to reply. If it is, I would appreciate hearing what you use today.

Async demo offer after interest:

> Here is the demo: https://www.byqalam.com/demo. The test is simple: use one non-confidential idea from your next real post and see whether Qalam gives you a draft worth editing. Free includes five AI posts per month; advanced voice and client workflows require a paid plan. I can help by text if you get stuck.

Do not promise free access to paid features. If someone wants a paid workflow, explain the published plan and reviewed Agency onboarding before the test.

## Days 5 to 9: observe a real task

Use text or a short screen recording with sample information. No founder face is needed.

1. Ask how the tester completed their last client post and how long revisions took.
2. Have them use an idea and writing context they have permission to share.
3. Let them generate, edit and save a draft. Observe rather than doing the entire job for them.
4. Ask what they would actually change before using it. Record the obstacle and elapsed time.
5. Ask when their next comparable task is. That date determines the return check.

Successful trial means the tester says the resulting draft is usable after their edits and saves or exports it. Generated text alone is insufficient evidence of value. Do not claim improved results or time savings without a comparison supplied by the tester.

Fix only a blocker that prevents this task, or a repeated obstacle observed in at least two sessions. Preserve unrelated functionality.

## Days 9 to 14: repeat and payment

Return-check draft:

> Did you use Qalam for your next post? If yes, what did you keep or change? If not, what did you use instead, and why?

Record whether the repeat happened before or after this reminder. Reminder-assisted use is different from an independent return.

Payment conversation after demonstrated value:

> You have now used Qalam for [verified task]. Would you like to continue on the published [appropriate plan]? The current price and included limits are here: https://www.byqalam.com/pricing. If it is not worth paying for, which part falls short of your current process?

Use existing checkout or the existing Agency application flow. Count payment only after confirmation, not a promise or checkout click. Do not discount reflexively or sell unlimited lifetime usage.

## Decision on day 14

| Observation | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer than five qualified trials | Demand remains uncertain. Improve recruitment or the invitation before judging the product. |
| Five trials, fewer than three usable drafts | Fix the dominant task or output problem. Do not increase acquisition volume. |
| Usable drafts, fewer than three repeat users | Investigate recurrence and advantage over the existing alternative. Do not add retention reminders as a substitute for usefulness. |
| Three repeat users, zero payments | Ask whether the paid capability solves a real problem; inspect checkout friction and plan fit. Free value alone does not prove willingness to pay. |
| Three repeat users and at least one confirmed payment | Repeat with ten more qualified prospects and observe the first cohort at day 28. This is an early signal, not product-market fit. |

Use the same five-person cohort when comparing trial and repeat counts. A repeat is another useful writing task on a different day within seven days of the first useful task. Mark users whose seven-day window has not elapsed as pending. At day 28, check useful activity during days 22 through 28 after their first useful task.

Track contacts separately from users. Never turn blank data into zeros. Use the CSV beside this document with anonymized tester IDs, not client documents, email addresses or private writing samples. Keep any real customer information in a private location outside the repository.

## What to add, reduce and automate

- Add now: structured customer feedback and the manual cohort tracker. No new service subscription is needed.
- Reduce now: feature expansion and marketing to several audiences at once. Do not delete capabilities without usage evidence.
- Defer: new AI agents, mass SEO pages, auto-comments, automated DMs and new dashboards. There is no repeated validated process to automate yet.
- Useful later automation: summarize consented feedback, draft a brand demo from sample data, and report confirmed funnel numbers. Build these when the manual task repeats enough to justify maintenance.
- Keep service promises within available time. Avoid selling managed writing packages until delivery capacity is established.
- Review existing provider usage before recruiting beyond this small cohort. A current free tier does not establish zero marginal cost at larger usage. Add no paid infrastructure for this experiment.
