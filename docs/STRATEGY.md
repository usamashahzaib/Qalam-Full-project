# Qalam Strategy

## Strategy Kernel

### Diagnosis

Talented professionals often have enough experience to be credible but lack a repeatable way to turn that experience into visible proof. Their resume, LinkedIn profile, content, and career narrative drift apart. Generic AI writing tools increase output but can weaken trust because they do not preserve a grounded professional record.

### Guiding Policy

Qalam will own the workflow from real experience to consistent professional proof. It will focus on Pakistan-first professionals who need career visibility, then expand through use cases where one accurate Career Vault improves several outcomes. Retention and demonstrated career value take priority over broad acquisition.

### Coherent Actions

1. Make the Career Vault the source of truth for claims, voice, evidence, and goals.
2. Deliver one useful artifact in the first session, starting with a writer draft or ATS assessment.
3. Connect each artifact to a visible score, next action, and saved evidence.
4. Measure activation and week-4 retained activation before scaling paid acquisition.
5. Use permissioned customer outcomes near pricing and avoid unsupported social proof.

## Product Analysis

### Current Strengths

- The product has a differentiated Career Visibility OS frame instead of a generic writing assistant frame.
- The public site shows real interface evidence and a low-commitment ATS assessment path.
- The application already combines content, resume, publishing, analytics, workspaces, and payments.
- Pricing is accessible for the local market and supports a free-to-paid path.

### Current Constraints

- Production activation and retention baselines do not yet exist.
- The product surface is broad, which can blur the first job to be done.
- Customer outcome stories and willingness-to-pay evidence are still thin.
- A multi-feature promise can increase time to first value unless onboarding routes by goal.

### Highest-Leverage Decision

Treat the first successful writer draft as signed-in activation and the ATS result as pre-account value. Measure which entry path produces the strongest week-4 retained activation before expanding acquisition.

## Job Stories

### Turn experience into a credible draft

When I know what I have achieved but struggle to express it, I want Qalam to use my real evidence and voice, so I can publish a credible draft without starting from a blank page.

Acceptance criteria:

1. The workflow accepts professional context or clearly shows what context is missing.
2. A useful first draft can be generated in the first session.
3. The result distinguishes grounded claims from unsupported claims.
4. The user can revise, score, save, and reuse the draft.
5. Failure preserves inputs and provides a specific recovery action.
6. Successful generation records activation once per workflow session.

### Check a resume before applying

When I am preparing an application and do not know why my resume may be rejected, I want a structured ATS assessment, so I can fix the highest-impact gaps before I apply.

Acceptance criteria:

1. The user can paste or upload a resume and optionally provide a job description.
2. The assessment explains its factors before asking for input.
3. The result includes an overall score, factor scores, and prioritized fixes.
4. A failed request preserves the resume and job description.
5. Completion records the assessment type, score band, and whether a job description was supplied.
6. The next action offers an honest path into the signed-in product.

### Keep every professional surface consistent

When my role, goals, or evidence changes, I want to update one trusted record, so I can keep my resume, LinkedIn, and content consistent without repeating the same work.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Saved evidence has a clear owner and edit path.
2. Generated artifacts identify the context they used.
3. Updating the source does not silently overwrite completed artifacts.
4. Users can review changes before publishing or exporting.
5. Workspaces maintain access boundaries.
6. The system records reuse so its relationship to retention can be measured.

## Monetization

### Value Metric

Price access by workflow capacity and professional leverage, not by raw AI tokens. Users understand drafts, resume reviews, publishing, workspaces, and managed help more readily than model usage.

### Segmentation

| Segment | Primary value | Offer direction |
|---|---|---|
| Explorer | Understand a current visibility gap | Free assessment and limited proof artifact |
| Solo professional | Create consistent proof regularly | Solo plan |
| Power user or consultant | Run several workflows and publish more often | Pro plan |
| Team or agency | Coordinate voices, approvals, and workspaces | Assisted plan after demand validation |

### Pricing Research Plan

Interview at least five people in each priority segment. Ask about the last problem, current workaround, cost of delay, budget owner, and purchase process before showing price. Record acceptable, expensive, prohibitively expensive, and suspiciously cheap price points. Keep the existing plans stable until the evidence supports a change.

## Scorecard Funnel

The ATS resume checker is the primary scorecard. It measures parsing, structure, relevance, evidence, clarity, and risk, then converts the result into prioritized action.

| Funnel stage | User promise | Event | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing | See whether a resume is ready before applying | resume_check_start | State method and privacy clearly |
| Assessment | Supply resume and optional target role | resume_check_start | Preserve inputs and show progress |
| Result | Receive score band and prioritized fixes | assessment_complete | Make the top fix unmistakable |
| Handoff | Continue with grounded resume workflows | signup start | Carry useful context with consent |
| Activation | Complete the first signed-in proof artifact | activation | Celebrate the artifact, not account creation |

## Strategic Tradeoffs

- Choose professional credibility over maximum output volume.
- Choose a Pakistan-first wedge over generic global messaging.
- Choose one coherent Career Vault over disconnected generators.
- Choose retained activation over top-of-funnel traffic.
- Choose transparent assessments over inflated scores and anxiety tactics.
