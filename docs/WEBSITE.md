# Website

## Sitemap

The public site includes the homepage, pricing, features, free tools, product pages, use cases, methodology, company, legal, and support content. The authenticated product begins after signup or login.

## Page Briefs

### / (home)

- Purpose and primary conversion action: create a free account
- Message: turn real professional experience into visible proof across resumes, LinkedIn, and content
- Direct CTA: Build my professional story free
- Transitional CTA: Check my resume free
- Copy blocks: promise, risk reversal, free useful result, illustrative transformation, live workflow previews, career toolkit, memory, three-step plan, methodology, pricing, FAQ, final CTA

## Audit Findings

| Issue | Severity (0-4) | Fix | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hero described four product categories before one outcome | 3 | Lead with visible proof and make the supporting products concrete | shipped |
| Transitional CTA led to another explanatory page | 3 | Route it to an immediate free tool on the same page | shipped |
| Referral promotion interrupted the prospect journey | 2 | Remove it from the homepage | shipped |
| Planned features competed with live capabilities | 2 | Remove the roadmap block from the homepage | shipped |
| Before and after copy could be mistaken for a customer result | 3 | Label it illustrative and use a neutral supplied-facts example | shipped |
| Genuine customer outcomes are missing | 4 | Collect permissioned named stories with measurable context | awaiting evidence |
| Funnel conversion baseline is missing | 4 | Marketing events are instrumented; collect a 28-day production baseline and add the activation event | awaiting production baseline |
| Authentication looked generic beside the marketing experience | 3 | Add a branded split shell, evidence-led trust copy, and privacy-aware password guardian | shipped |
| Mobile footer required a long scroll through every link group | 2 | Collapse link groups into accessible native accordions on mobile | shipped |
| Local side-by-side development sent app CTAs to the other server | 3 | Keep login, signup, dashboard, settings, and career links relative in development | shipped |

## Lead Capture

The free resume checker is the no-account value path. Account creation remains the primary product conversion.
