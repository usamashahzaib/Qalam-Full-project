# Qalam Brand System

## Brand Idea

Qalam turns lived experience into credible professional proof. The brand should feel like a modern editorial instrument: precise, calm, premium, and grounded in work that actually happened.

## Brand Character

| Attribute | Express through | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Credible | Specific claims, visible method, restrained confidence | Inflated promises and fake metrics |
| Premium | Deep teal, warm paper, gold used as an accent | Glossy gradients and excessive glow |
| Human | Real language, editorial rhythm, visible user control | Chatbot clichés and synthetic enthusiasm |
| Focused | One primary action, short labels, deliberate whitespace | Dense feature walls and equal-weight CTAs |
| Grounded | Evidence, source context, transparent scoring | Unsupported authority signals |

## Core Message

Category: Career Visibility OS.

Promise: Turn experience into proof.

Explanation: Qalam uses your real experience to keep your resume, LinkedIn presence, content, and career narrative credible and consistent.

## Voice

Use direct sentences, concrete nouns, and observable outcomes. Prefer "Check your resume before you apply" to "Unlock next-level career success." Explain limits, privacy, availability, and next steps plainly. Do not use generic phrases such as seamless, revolutionary, powerful, game-changing, elevate, unlock your potential, or AI-powered when a precise capability can be named.

## Color System

| Role | Direction | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep teal | Navigation, primary actions, strong surfaces |
| Accent | Muted gold | Key emphasis, rules, selected details, never body text on white |
| Canvas | Warm paper | Editorial marketing surfaces and calm backgrounds |
| Surface | White and warm neutral | Cards, forms, product work areas |
| Text | Near-black and deep teal | Long-form readability and hierarchy |
| Status | Semantic green, amber, red, and blue | Pair every color with text or an icon |

Contrast requirements: body text reaches WCAG AA, focus rings remain visible on every surface, and gold is tested in its actual foreground and background pairing before use.

## Typography

Jakarta is the interface and body family. Cormorant supplies short editorial emphasis, not dense UI copy. Headings are decisive and compact. Body measures remain readable, with at least 16 pixel text and relaxed line height.

## Logo

The gold Q ring and ink-drop detail represent a writing instrument, continuity, and transformation from thought to proof. Preserve clear space, proportions, and high contrast. Do not add effects, redraw the drop, place the mark on noisy imagery, or use the gold mark on a low-contrast surface.

## Image Direction

Prefer interface evidence, editorial still life, paper texture, refined materials, and real customer proof with permission. Avoid generic laptop mockups, anonymous corporate teams, floating AI orbs, fake dashboards, and decorative people who imply endorsements.

## Motion

- Motion clarifies state, causality, or continuity.
- Routine transitions use 120 to 250 milliseconds.
- Primary actions respond immediately and settle without bounce-heavy spectacle.
- A completed proof artifact is the signature moment: reveal result, score, evidence, and next action together.
- Reduced-motion mode preserves every state change without travel or repeated reveals.

## Interface Principles

1. Make the user's current state and next action obvious.
2. Prefer recognition to recall by showing context, recent artifacts, and decoded labels.
3. Preserve input after errors and explain the specific recovery action.
4. Keep all essential mobile targets at least 44 by 44 pixels.
5. Use platform conventions for navigation, forms, focus, and back behavior.
6. Give users control over generation, editing, publishing, and deletion.

## Governance

- Product owns message accuracy and availability claims.
- Design owns tokens, accessibility, logo use, and motion.
- Marketing owns campaign consistency and permissioned proof.
- Engineering prevents unapproved copy and token drift through reusable components and automated checks.
