## Key Decisions

1. The repeatable core action is creating or improving a grounded proof artifact.
2. Writer draft completion is signed-in activation.
3. ATS assessment completion is pre-account value and remains a separate event.
4. Career Vault investment is the weakest Hook phase.
5. Week-4 retained activation is the current One Metric That Matters.
6. Paid acquisition is gated until the baseline and cohort curve exist.
7. Recognition follows meaningful progress. Qalam will not use pressure streaks, hidden loss, or forced prompts.
