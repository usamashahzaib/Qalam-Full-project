# Qalam vs Teal ATS workflow

Reviewed: 2026-08-27

Sources: [Teal Resume Builder](https://www.tealhq.com/tools/resume-builder), [Teal Resume Builder guide](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/9508933-getting-started-resume-builder)

## Company overview

Teal positions its product as a broad job-search workspace with an ATS resume builder, job matching, application tracking, cover letters, and LinkedIn tools. Its official resume-builder page promotes unlimited resume versions and downloads on the free plan, with advanced analysis and AI features in paid tiers.

Qalam positions the ATS workflow inside a Career Visibility OS. The free public checker requires no account. Resume Studio then connects the user's saved evidence, career context, professional profile, and target job.

## Quick comparison

| Capability | Qalam | Teal | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-account ATS check | Full public checker with no sign-in | Account-led builder flow | Qalam |
| Free resume volume | One generated resume each month | Unlimited resume versions and downloads | Teal |
| Evidence control | Career Vault and truth-preserving generation | Resume import, editing, and AI suggestions | Qalam for grounded context |
| Job targeting | Exact job description targeting | Job matching and keyword guidance | Tie |
| Templates | 12 ATS-safe templates | 100+ templates promoted on the official page | Teal |
| Career context reuse | Resume, profile, publishing context, applications, and matching in one workspace | Resume and broader job-search workflow | Different emphasis |

## Where Qalam wins

- The ATS check delivers practical value before account creation.
- The handoff can connect the result to saved career evidence without sending resume contents into analytics.
- Signal Match reuses grounded professional context while keeping publication behind explicit consent.
- The product promise rejects invented experience and unsupported keywords.

## Where Teal wins

- Teal promotes unlimited free resume creation and downloads. Qalam must not imply a volume advantage.
- Teal promotes a larger template library and a mature job-tracking workflow.
- Teal has broader public social proof and a longer-established resume-builder category presence.

## Common objections and responses

| Prospect says | Respond with |
|---|---|
| "Teal gives me unlimited resumes." | "Use Teal if unlimited volume is the deciding factor. Qalam gives you a no-account ATS diagnosis, then builds from evidence and career context you control." |
| "Why create an account after the check?" | "The account holds editable resume versions, Career Vault evidence, job targeting, and exports. The checker result itself remains free and account-free." |
| "Will Qalam copy my resume into analytics?" | "No. The funnel accepts only fixed source, placement, and destination tokens. Resume and job-description contents are excluded from the event schema." |

## Landmines to plant

- How important is a useful ATS diagnosis before account creation?
- How do you keep AI rewrites tied to facts the candidate can defend?
- Can one saved source of truth support resumes, applications, LinkedIn positioning, and peer discovery?
- What data crosses the marketing analytics boundary during the resume handoff?

## Win and loss patterns

- Qalam tends to win when evidence control, cross-workflow context, and pre-signup utility matter.
- Qalam tends to lose when unlimited free resume volume or template breadth is the primary buying criterion.
- The key differentiator is a connected, consent-aware career context rather than resume generation alone.
