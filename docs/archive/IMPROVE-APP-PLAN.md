## Key Decisions

- The daily job is to keep one useful result, decision, or lesson before it is forgotten.
- Use Today's Win as the human label. Evidence remains the formal product term where verification matters.
- Keep streaks forgiving. Missing a day never deletes saved work or blocks progress.
- The 13-stage path represents meaningful product progress, not time spent online.
- Future rewards must provide real utility, such as bonus smart comments or an extra review.
- No random loot boxes, fake scarcity, paid streak repair, or rewards for idle screen time.
