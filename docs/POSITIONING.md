# Positioning

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)

- Character: a serious professional who wants their experience to be understood and trusted
- External problem: resumes, LinkedIn, and content tell disconnected stories
- Internal problem: they know they have substance but struggle to present it clearly
- Philosophical problem: credible experience should not be overlooked because it was expressed poorly
- Guide empathy: Qalam starts from the work and evidence the user already has
- Guide authority: transparent methods, product-level previews, and controlled publishing
- Plan: assess the evidence, align the professional story, improve the surfaces that opportunities see
- Direct CTA: Build my professional story free
- Transitional CTA: Check my resume free
- Failure: capable people remain generic, inconsistent, or difficult to evaluate
- Success: one credible story makes them easier to trust, shortlist, and remember

## One-Liner

Qalam is a career visibility workspace that turns real experience into a trusted LinkedIn presence, credible content, and stronger resumes.

## LinkedIn Content System

- Positioning: profile, target audience, expertise, and recurring content pillars reinforce one defensible professional identity.
- Authority content: teach from supplied expertise and proof.
- Personal content: build recognition through true moments, beliefs, and behind-the-scenes lessons.
- Offer content: connect a real audience problem to a relevant service or next step without sounding like an ad.
- Trust constraints: never invent proof, never use engagement bait, and never promise reach or algorithmic distribution.
