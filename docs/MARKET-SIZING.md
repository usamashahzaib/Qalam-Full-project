# Market Sizing

## Decision Use

This model is for prioritization and planning. It is not a forecast. Registered LinkedIn members are an upper-bound signal, not active users or buyers.

## Source Facts

- LinkedIn reports more than 1.3 billion global members.
- DataReportal reports that LinkedIn advertising resources indicated 18.0 million registered members in Pakistan in late 2025, up 28.6 percent year over year. The source warns that registered members are not directly comparable with monthly active users.
- Pakistan Bureau of Statistics publishes Labour Force Survey 2024-25 data that can support later occupation and income segmentation.

Sources:

- https://news.linkedin.com/about-us
- https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan
- https://www.pbs.gov.pk/labour-force-statistics/

## Bottom-Up Model

Assumptions use a blended paid monthly revenue of PKR 900, between the current Solo and Pro price points after mix and discounts.

| Layer | Calculation | Annual revenue opportunity |
|---|---|---:|
| Registered-member ceiling | 18,000,000 x PKR 900 x 12 | PKR 194.4 billion |
| Serviceable audience hypothesis | 900,000, or 5 percent of the ceiling, x PKR 900 x 12 | PKR 9.72 billion |
| Initial reachable segment | 180,000, or 1 percent of the ceiling, x PKR 900 x 12 | PKR 1.944 billion |
| Year-1 operating target | 1,000 average paid accounts x PKR 900 x 12 | PKR 10.8 million |
| Year-3 operating target | 10,000 average paid accounts x PKR 900 x 12 | PKR 108 million |

## Interpretation

The 18 million figure is not TAM by itself. It only bounds a professional-network audience. The useful market is the share with an urgent visibility job, sufficient ability to pay, and a reachable acquisition path. The 5 percent and 1 percent filters are explicit hypotheses that must be replaced with observed conversion and segment data.

## Validation Plan

1. Measure unique Pakistan-based assessment users by job goal.
2. Measure assessment-to-signup, signup-to-activation, and activation-to-paid conversion.
3. Estimate reachable accounts for each channel using real response and conversion rates.
4. Replace the blended revenue assumption with realized monthly revenue per paid account.
5. Recalculate quarterly and keep a base, downside, and upside case.
