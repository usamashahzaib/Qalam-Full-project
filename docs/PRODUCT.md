# Product Growth

## Vision

Qalam becomes the trusted Career Visibility OS for people whose experience is stronger than their current professional presence. It converts grounded evidence into a coherent resume, LinkedIn presence, content voice, and career narrative while leaving the user in control of every claim.

## Hook Model

| Phase | Qalam loop | Strength | Decision |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Internal trigger | Concern that valuable experience is invisible, inconsistent, or poorly expressed | 7/10 | Use real uncertainty, never manufactured anxiety |
| Action | Open a current workflow and generate or improve one proof artifact | 6/10 | Route the first session to one job, not the full product |
| Variable reward | Discover a stronger framing, score improvement, or evidence gap | 7/10 | Prefer mastery and useful discovery over random rewards |
| Investment | Save evidence, voice, goals, feedback, and approved artifacts to the Career Vault | 5/10 | Make saved evidence visibly improve the next workflow |

Weakest phase: investment. The highest-leverage retention test is to show exactly how newly saved evidence improves the next artifact and reduces repeated setup.

Manipulation Matrix decision: the maker would use the product, believes grounded professional proof improves the user's life, and does not need deceptive anxiety, hidden costs, or loss-aversion streaks.

Starter Step: complete an ATS assessment or generate one grounded draft with the smallest useful context. Celebration moment: reveal the completed artifact, its score, the strongest evidence used, and one next action.

## Discovery Cadence

- One recent-event interview each week, with a monthly synthesis.
- Recruit from completed ATS assessments, newly activated accounts, retained accounts, and churned paid accounts.
- Ask about the last real attempt, what happened before it, current alternatives, constraints, and what they did next.
- Store facts and observed commitments separately from opinions and feature requests.

## Team Model

Use outcome ownership with continuous discovery alongside delivery. The team receives a problem and success measure, then evaluates value, usability, feasibility, and viability before committing a feature. Feature requests without recent evidence remain candidates, not roadmap commitments.

## Intrinsic Motivation Audit

| Dimension | Score | Finding | Action |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Autonomy | 8/10 | Users retain editing and publishing control | Keep onboarding and prompts skippable |
| Mastery | 7/10 | Scores and revisions can show improvement | Connect progress to specific professional skills and evidence |
| Purpose | 8/10 | Career visibility is tied to meaningful work outcomes | Keep outcomes factual and user-defined |

Meaningless points are not recommended. A staged path can work when every step represents real professional progress and every reward has genuine utility. Streaks remain optional and forgiving. Missing a day never deletes progress.

## Qalam Sprint

The proposed retention layer is a 13-stage professional progress path inspired by the clarity of a game map, not by gambling mechanics.

| Mechanic | Qalam version | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Short levels | One useful action such as saving a win, improving a profile, publishing, or tracking an application | No reward for idle time or opening the app |
| Race progress | A visible 13-stage personal path | Compete with your previous state, not a fabricated leaderboard |
| Lives and recovery | Continue after a missed day with all saved work intact | No paid repair and no shame message |
| Currency | Future bonus smart-comment or review credits | Clear balance, predictable earning rules, and no chance-based purchase |
| Difficulty help | Break a hard task into a smaller suggested action | Never weaken quality or invent professional evidence |
| Variable reward | A useful prompt, stronger framing, or occasional utility credit after real work | Reward value, not compulsive time spent |

## Growth Gates

- Retention gate: do not scale paid acquisition until a complete 28-day activation baseline and week-4 cohort exist.
- Habit signal: at least 5 percent of users return unprompted for the core workflow, tracked separately from reminder-driven return.
- Ethical gate: every engagement tactic must create value the maker would personally use and honestly recommend.
