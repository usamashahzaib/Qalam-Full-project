# Qalam - Cold Outreach Lead-Gen Kit

Get leads now, without waiting for followers. Every template below is honest - no fake growth, no fake claims. It sells Qalam's real value (voice memory, hooks, archive, agency workspaces, comment generator) and offers something useful up front.

Send from a real person (you or a team member) on LinkedIn DM or email. Real name, real face beats any brand account for cold outreach.

---

## 1. Who to target (ICP) - pick 2 to start

Your repo already tells you who Qalam is for. Start narrow - two segments convert faster than "everyone."

**A. Ghostwriting / content agencies (1-15 people)**
Their pain: managing 6+ clients' voices, revision churn, "this doesn't sound like me." Qalam's agency workspaces + voice memory solve exactly this. Highest willingness to pay.

**B. Solo consultants / coaches building inbound on LinkedIn**
Their pain: inconsistent posting, blank page, sounding generic. Qalam removes friction while keeping their voice. Fast decision, low price sensitivity if you show value.

**C. Founders (seed / bootstrapped SaaS)**
Their pain: know their market, no time, posts sound outsourced. Secondary segment - longer sales cycle.

**How to find them on LinkedIn:**
- Search titles: "ghostwriter", "LinkedIn ghostwriter", "content agency founder", "personal brand strategist", "fractional CMO".
- Filter by people who post 2+ times a week (they already value LinkedIn - easiest to convert).
- Look at who comments on big LinkedIn-creator posts (Aqsa, Imtisal, etc.) - that's a pre-qualified pool.
- Aim for 20 targeted contacts/day, not 200 spray-and-pray.

---

## 2. The rule for every message

1. No pitch in message one. Lead with a specific observation or a free useful thing.
2. One sentence about them, then one about the value, then a soft question.
3. Never claim results you don't have. "Built for X" is honest; "helped 100 people get X" is not (unless true).
4. Short. If it looks like a wall of text, it's deleted.

---

## 3. LinkedIn DM sequence (agencies)

**DM 1 - connect note (no pitch):**
> Hi {name} - saw you run {agency}. I'm building a tool for ghostwriting teams around keeping each client's voice separate instead of everything drifting to the same template. Would love to connect and learn how you handle voice consistency across accounts.

**DM 2 - after they accept (value, still no hard pitch):**
> Thanks for connecting. Quick one - at how many clients did keeping distinct voices start getting hard for your team? That "everything sounds the same" drift is the exact problem I'm working on, and I'm collecting how agencies deal with it now.

**DM 3 - soft offer:**
> That lines up with what I keep hearing. I've built Qalam around per-client voice memory + approval flow so quality doesn't flatten as you scale. Happy to give you free early access and get your honest take - no strings. Want me to send a link?

## 4. LinkedIn DM sequence (consultants)

**DM 1 - connect note:**
> Hi {name} - really liked your post on {topic}. I'm building a tool that helps consultants post consistently on LinkedIn without the blank-page grind or sounding generic. Connecting because you're clearly doing this well already.

**DM 2:**
> Genuine question - what's the real reason posting slips for you when you're busy? Time, or the blank page? I'm building around removing exactly that friction and want to make sure I'm solving the right one.

**DM 3 - soft offer:**
> Makes sense. Qalam keeps your voice (learns from your own posts) and kills the blank page, so consistency stops depending on your mood. Want free early access to try it on your next few posts?

## 6. Follow-up (once, 3-4 days later, if no reply)

> Hi {name} - floating this back up in case it got buried. No pressure at all. If voice consistency across clients isn't a pain for you, totally fine to ignore. If it is, the early-access offer stands.

---

## 7. The founding-user offer (real scarcity, no fake anything)

Use this as the "close" in DMs and on your landing page:

> **Founding 20:** the first 20 users get {lifetime discount / 3 months free / half price} and direct input into what we build. When 20 are in, this closes.

This works because it's true. Keep a real count. When it's full, it's full. That honest scarcity converts better than any fake "500 users love us" badge - and it can't blow up in your face later.

---

## 8. Daily rhythm (what actually gets leads)

- 20 targeted connection requests/DMs a day (quality list, not random).
- 10 thoughtful comments a day on your ICP's posts - this warms them before you ever DM.
- Post 3x/week from a real face (use `../founder profile/10-founder-profile-posts.md`).
- Track replies in a simple sheet: contacted / replied / trial / paid.

Consistency here for 3-4 weeks beats any one-time trick. Real outreach + real posts + a real offer = real pipeline.
