# LinkedIn content pack

Open CONTENT-CALENDAR.md for the posting order and final captions.

- Carousels: upload the PDF as a LinkedIn document post. The PPTX is editable. Individual PNG slides are included for review or reuse.
- Posters: upload the matching PNG with its caption. The poster-pack PPTX is editable.
- Text posts: publish the caption directly without an image.

The copy uses only facts present in the supplied LinkedIn profile. No new employer, client, revenue, team-size, or personal-story claims were added.
