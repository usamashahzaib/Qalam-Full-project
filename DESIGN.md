# Qalam Design Contract

## Product Position

Qalam is a Career Visibility OS for professionals who want to be found, trusted, and hired.

## Visual Direction

Premium, calm, credible, evidence-led. Keep the established teal, gold, warm-white, and zinc system. Use editorial hierarchy, generous spacing, restrained motion, and clear product-state labels.

## Information Architecture

Public pages must connect four engines:

1. LinkedIn Positioning
2. Content Intelligence
3. ATS Career Engine
4. Professional Growth

Authenticated product areas must preserve one shared Career Vault across profile audits, content, resumes, job matching, recruiter visibility, cohorts, and add-ons.

## Interface Rules

- One primary action per section.
- Use cards only for real grouping or comparison.
- Mark unavailable capabilities as planned, beta, or controlled rollout.
- Never present recommendations as measured LinkedIn data.
- Never claim guaranteed reach, interviews, revenue, or recruiter activity.
- Use sentence case.
- Keep touch targets at least 44px.
- Keep visible focus states and semantic controls.
- Support 360px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px widths.
- Respect reduced motion.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with outcome, then explain mechanism.
- Use plain English.
- Prefer specific nouns and verbs.
- Separate measured signals, supplied evidence, inferred patterns, and recommendations.
- Do not use fake urgency, unsupported comparisons, or invented scarcity.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes.

## Commercial Truth

- Billing is quarterly.
- Pakistan-facing pricing is PKR-first.
- Add-ons must show unit, quantity, and total before checkout.
- Free allowances, paid entitlements, and coming-soon features must match backend enforcement.

## Quality Bar

Every public claim must match a working route, verified capability, or explicit release-state label. Every app action must expose success, failure, loading, empty, and permission states.
