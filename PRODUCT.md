# Qalam Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

Qalam serves founders, consultants, operators, marketing teams, agencies, HR leaders, and other serious professionals who publish on LinkedIn. They need to turn expertise into consistent content without losing their personal voice or rebuilding context every session.

## Product Purpose

Qalam is a premium LinkedIn publishing system. It learns from a user's posts and edits, preserves drafts and reusable content assets, supports scheduling and publishing workflows, and compounds context over time. Success means users publish consistently, sound like themselves, and treat their archive as a durable business asset.

## Brand Personality

Premium, serious, editorial. Qalam should feel calm, confident, direct, and operationally trustworthy. The interface should communicate expert judgment without becoming clinical or corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI writer landing pages, chatbot-first positioning, fake analytics, toy or emoji-heavy UI, neon AI gradients, glassmorphism, excessive glow, repetitive card grids, and decorative motion. Avoid any treatment that makes Qalam feel disposable, gimmicky, or indistinguishable from a generic SaaS template.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product and its current operational state.
2. Make voice memory and content compounding tangible.
3. Keep one clear primary action at every decision point.
4. Prefer credible evidence and plain language over marketing spectacle.
5. Preserve familiar product patterns so the interface disappears into the publishing task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard and screen-reader use, visible focus, reduced motion, 200 percent zoom, readable mobile type, adequate contrast, and touch targets appropriate for one-handed use.
